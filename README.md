---

## 🎯 Why This Project Exists
In Indian schools, teachers guide students closely step-by-step. When students move into undergraduate honors programs like **B.Sc. (Hons.) Physics at Delhi University**, they suddenly navigate dense mathematical derivations, multiple reference textbooks, lab practicals, and independent exam prep on their own.

Built by an **AI Enthusiast & Father** for his son and fellow DU Physics students across all colleges, **DU Physics Mentor** turns AI into a patient, syllabus-grounded Professor of Physics—100% open and free for everyone.

---

## ✨ What Students Get Inside
- **📚 Complete 6-Semester Roadmap (DSC-1 to DSC-18):** Chapter-by-chapter Professor notes, derivations, formulas, plus DSE, Minor (GE), SEC, VAC, and AEC guides.
- **🔍 Internal Topic & Keyword Finder:** Search any physics concept (e.g., *Coriolis force, Wheatstone bridge, Schrodinger equation*) to jump straight to where it appears across notes, labs, and PYQs.
- **🧪 Practicals & Lab Hub (Sem I–VI):** Semester-wise experiments, marks breakdown, and viva-voce preparation tips.
- **🎥 Verified Lecture Index:** Direct links to **NPTEL (IIT)** and **MIT OpenCourseWare** video playlists mapped to DU papers.
- **🃏 Active-Recall Flashcards & DU Sample Papers:** Self-paced revision cards and full 90-mark UGCF-style sample papers with model solutions.
- **📝 DU PYQ Bank & AI Professor Grader:** High-frequency Delhi University exam questions plus an AI grader that checks derivations, boundary conditions, and dimensional consistency.
- **📖 Textbook ROI Guide:** Clear *Buy vs. Borrow/Library vs. Reference Only* recommendations with AI Professor ratings.

## 🗺️ Covered Curriculum (DU NEP UGCF-2022)

| Semester | Core Physics Papers (Discipline Specific Core) | Practicals & Lab Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Semester 1** | DSC-1: Mathematical Physics I • DSC-2: Mechanics • DSC-3: Waves & Oscillations | Error analysis, pendulum, spring-mass, programming |
| **Semester 2** | DSC-4: Mathematical Physics II • DSC-5: Electricity & Magnetism • DSC-6: Electrical Circuit Analysis | RC/RL/RLC circuits, Wheatstone bridge, potentiometer |
| **Semester 3** | DSC-7: Mathematical Physics III • DSC-8: Thermal Physics • DSC-9: Light & Matter | Logic gates, flip-flops, optics experiments |
| **Semester 4** | DSC-10: Modern Physics • DSC-11: Solid State Physics • DSC-12: Analog Systems & Applications | *e/m* measurement, black-body radiation, transistors |
| **Semester 5** | DSC-13: Electromagnetic Theory • DSC-14: Quantum Mechanics I • DSC-15: Digital Systems & Applications | Hall effect, magnetic susceptibility, microwave/EM labs |
| **Semester 6** | DSC-16: Statistical Mechanics • DSC-17: Atomic & Molecular Physics • DSC-18: Nuclear & Particle Physics | Nuclear counting, spectroscopy, statistical analysis |
